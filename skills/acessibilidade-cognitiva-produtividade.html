Software engineering centered on cognitive ergonomics and neurodiversity (Autism Spectrum Disorder — ASD, ADHD, and Dyslexia) in compliance with W3C WCAG 2.1 AAA guidelines, offline neural speech synthesis for assistive reading and audiobooks (QTextToSpeech and Win32 SAPI5/OneCore), full keyboard navigability with high-contrast focus rings and semantic screen reader integration (MSAA, UI Automation, NVDA, and Narrator), dyslexia-specialized typography, trigger-free sensory comfort presets, asynchronous digital library cataloging (PDF, EPUB, MOBI, DJVU, and CBZ), academic evaluation scheduling, and native integration of scientific priority management, deep focus, and active learning methodologies (4-quadrant Eisenhower Decision Matrix with Drag-and-Drop, Pomodoro Focus Cycles, and Feynman Technique).
