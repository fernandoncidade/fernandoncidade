Analytical mathematical modeling and automated code compliance: wind actions on buildings and steel structures (ABNT NBR 6123:2023 and NBR 8800:2024 — V0 isopleth maps, embedded FAB weather station database, S1/S2/S3 factors, external/internal pressure coefficients Cpe/Cpi, dominant openings, linear frame/purlin reactions compatible with Ftool/SAP2000/CYPE/Eberick, global drag, and overturning moment), slope stability and soil mechanics (ABNT NBR 11682:2009 and NBR 6122:2022 — method of slices limit equilibrium via iterative Simplified Bishop, Fellenius, and Simplified Janbu with parallel parametric grid search over slip centers/radii, multi-layer Mohr-Coulomb stratigraphy, water table/pore pressure, continuous Safety Factor heatmap, Rankine and Coulomb active/passive earth pressures, and Hansen/Meyerhof ultimate foundation bearing capacity), reinforced concrete and L/T cantilever and gravity/gabion retaining wall design (ABNT NBR 6118:2023 — ductile Domain 3 simple flexure, shear resistance VRd1, crack width control wk, and commercial rebar detailing for stem, toe, and heel), and experimental and rational Portland cement concrete mix proportioning via the IPT/EPUSP Method (ABNT NBR 12655:2022, NBR 7211:2022, NBR 7212:2025, and NBR 8953:2015 — Abrams, Lyse, and Molinari laws, binary coarse aggregate packing, dry mortar content alpha, 4-quadrant dosage nomogram, field batching conversion with moisture/bulking correction, gauging box sizing, and per-m³ cost analysis).

Modelagem automatizada da velocidade característica Vk, pressão dinâmica q e combinações críticas de pressão externa e interna em galpões industriais e coberturas, gerando carregamentos distribuídos prontos para análise estrutural e wireframe 3D interativo.
